## Laboratory

The lab has dedicated research space configured for the full development workflow: molecular reporter design, nanopore assay development, microfluidic cartridge prototyping, electronic integration, and analytical testing. Fabrication resources include soft lithography for microfluidic prototyping, 3D printing and laser micromachining for precision mechanical parts, and dedicated electronics workstations for multichannel instrument development. Wet-lab capabilities support nucleic acid handling, bioconjugation, thermal cycling, PCR, and gel electrophoresis.

**Biosafety.** The laboratory is approved for Biosafety Level 2 (BSL-2) research and supports safe handling of de-identified human specimens in designated BSL-2 rooms with a certified Class II biosafety cabinet. All personnel maintain current IU Environmental Health & Safety training in biosafety, recombinant DNA, and bloodborne pathogens.

**Office.** Office space for the PI, postdoctoral fellows, and graduate students is located next to the laboratory.
